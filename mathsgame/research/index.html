Three points to carry into the discussion

Transition terms vary. The Irish custody summary allows issuer termination on three months’ notice; US ordinary termination uses 180 business days.

Provider economics need the fee letters. Sponsor charges and published depository tariffs do not establish JPMorgan’s negotiated bullion-custody price.

Gross flows matter for capacity. Daily share changes are net measures. Gross orders by location and settlement time are still needed to size float and throughput.

What the trend says

SLV: growth followed by a sharp slowdown

Gross metal flows rose from 507.0m oz in 2024 to 680.7m oz in 2025 (+34.3%). Q1 2026 then reached 223.6m oz; Q2 fell to 68.5m oz (-69.4% quarter on quarter).

Direction matters as much as total activity

SLV H1 2026 gross activity was only 1.5% below H1 2025, but redemptions exceeded creations by 47.7m oz. IAU also saw net withdrawals; IAUM saw net additions. A single assumption about flow direction would miss these differences.

The Irish series have different growth patterns

Annual gross flows increased 35.4% for silver, 68.0% for platinum and 27.1% for palladium. Unhedged gold declined 12.7%; the two hedged gold series grew from much smaller bases.

What we would request to size the service

Obtain daily and intraday gross orders by fund, authorised participant and delivery vault. Calculate peak and 95th/99th-percentile metal demand, consecutive-day withdrawals, settlement delays and same-day offsets. Points that cannot be presented as established facts

  • JPMorgan’s negotiated SLV storage and handling rates: the fee letters/invoices remain private. Published COMEX maximum tariffs are comparators, not SLV prices.
  • Custody versus trading title: trustee-authorised release or bar selection does not automatically transfer the client’s metal to Citi’s trading desk. The ownership and payment arrangements need their own documented route. Current ETF bars have not been shown to be on CME warrant.
  • Capacity and execution readiness: discussions with major providers and available space are desk-supplied commercial evidence. Signed capacity, throughput, insurance and transition terms remain needed; the public filings do not prove them.
  • Tariff and capture scenarios: 10%/20% tariffs, 85% capture, deployable fractions, $0.08/oz shipment and 10% cash initial margin are assumptions. Neither the filing audit nor the calculations validate tariff probabilities or expected profit.
