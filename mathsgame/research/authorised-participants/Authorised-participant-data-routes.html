The extract to request

25 October 2024–23 October 2026, the two-year window used in this research.

  • Fund ticker and identifier
  • Unique order ID; AP legal entity or anonymised participant code
  • Creation/redemption direction, kept separately as gross quantities
  • Order and acceptance date/time
  • Contractual and actual settlement dates
  • Status: accepted, settled, cancelled, rejected, failed or amended
  • Basket count, share quantity and actual fine troy ounces
  • US/UK receipt/delivery location and vault where recorded
  • Cancellations, reversals, rebooks and cash adjustments
  • Coverage: own AP, client subset or all APs; missing days and retention

What it would let us calculate

  • Separate gross creations and redemptions by order and settlement date
  • Daily mean, median, mode, maximum, minimum, zero days and 90th/95th/99th percentiles
  • Same-day two-way activity and netting that shares-outstanding changes conceal
  • Settlement lag, failures and stress-day concentration
  • US/UK distribution where the source actually identifies location
Why gross matters

Ten baskets created and eight redeemed leave the same net change as two baskets created and none redeemed. The operational workload and metal movements can be very different.

A primary order does not necessarily require an external shipment. Title transfers, substitutions, relocations and metal sales for expenses need separate classification.
