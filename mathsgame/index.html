Citi inventory and new orders

Size eligible Citi-owned stock against gross creation demand and stressed settlement flows. Allocate incoming silver against accepted orders and process redemptions under the agreed instructions.

Do not treat a net daily share change as the day’s gross metal requirement.

EXISTING HOLDINGS

Phased bar transfers

Agree vault slots and batches with the incumbent. Match bar records before dispatch; receive, check and reconcile each batch at the new location.

Use in-place custody transfer or approved substitution where it reduces physical handling.

Daily control point: bar identity + fine ounces + legal ownership + receiving confirmation. Resolve breaks before the next transfer batch.

Operational resilience built in by design

Custody controls that support growth and remain effective during stress

Build cross-training into the business resilience strategy. An expanded operating network allows Citi to redeploy trained staff when volumes surge, teams are absent or a site is disrupted.

PHYSICAL ASSURANCE

Counts and inspection

Propose an annual independent full bar count at every custody location, with interim random counts and enhanced quarterly checks for BlackRock.

Agree inspection access for BlackRock and BNY Mellon, and evidence how count exceptions are closed.

INVENTORY RECORDS

Daily bar-level reconciliation

Connect vault records to Citi’s custody ledger through APIs. Match refiner, serial number, year, gross weight, assay, fine ounces and location.

Publish the daily bar list and deliver controlled records to BNY Mellon and BlackRock.

INSURANCE AND SECURITY

Coverage through every hand-off

Align vault, in-transit and handling cover with actual values and concentrations. Set clear responsibility at dispatch, transit and receipt.

Document limits, exclusions, claims procedures and the controls over physical access.

PEOPLE AND SYSTEMS

Cover for critical functions

Cross-train allocation, reconciliation and settlement teams across locations. Maintain alternative communications, recovery access and documented handovers.

Exercise staff redeployment and site disruption plans using peak-volume scenarios.

Automation that supports the custody record

Explore the handling and scanning capabilities described for the London extension. The objective is faster receipt checks, fewer manual entries and a complete audit trail from the vault to the trustee.

Operating sequence: vault record → Citi reconciliation → trustee confirmation → BlackRock reporting.

These are proposed service standards for the Citi mandate. The capacity brief’s technology descriptions are development opportunities, not a verified description of installed systems.

Transfer quantities and fees

The photographs mix approximately 70.2m oz in New York with approximately 416,000 bars for a much larger pool. This presentation uses the verified 9 October scopes above. “Three receipts a day” does not determine a transfer duration without payload, working days and vault throughput. The source’s 12–24 month / 2–3 year / 3–5+ year estimates therefore do not establish a delivery schedule.

The brief’s $10 per bar load-out assumption and $4.16m total are not adopted as JPMorgan’s SLV price. CME notice MKR 11-20-25C publishes a maximum silver delivery-out charge of $35 per bar for JPMorgan, effective 1 March 2026. That is a COMEX comparator, not the negotiated SLV or London price. The brief’s $500,000 logistics per 13,000t and silver 20t / gold 3–5t load limits likewise need route-specific quotes and insurance terms.

Custody and ownership

The existing SLV agreement requires prior trustee approval, with sponsor consultation, for substitution; fine weight must be preserved. Its location clause covers England and New York, with other locations by agreement and sponsor approval. Custody and CME warrant registration are separate: no assumption is made that current SLV bars are on warrant. Citi is the proposed successor custodian.
